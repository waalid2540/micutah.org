# Recipe 1: Lentil Soup (Shorbat Adas)
## The Prophet's ﷺ Favorite

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 6 | **Calories:** 180

### Ingredients:
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups water or vegetable broth
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp turmeric
- Salt & pepper to taste
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

### Instructions:
1. Sauté onion in olive oil until soft (3 min)
2. Add garlic, cumin, turmeric - stir 1 minute
3. Add lentils, carrots, and water
4. Bring to boil, then simmer 20 minutes
5. Blend until smooth (or leave chunky)
6. Add lemon juice, season to taste
7. Garnish with cilantro

**Tip:** Make a big batch and freeze portions for busy nights!

# Recipe 2: Grilled Chicken Salad
## Light & Energizing

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 4 | **Calories:** 320

### Ingredients:
- 2 chicken breasts
- 4 cups mixed greens
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
- ¼ cup feta cheese (optional)

### Dressing:
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp honey
- Salt & pepper

### Instructions:
1. Season chicken with salt, pepper, paprika
2. Grill 6-7 minutes per side until cooked
3. Let rest 5 minutes, then slice
4. Arrange greens on plates
5. Top with vegetables and chicken
6. Whisk dressing ingredients, drizzle over salad

**Tip:** Prep chicken the night before for faster iftar!

# Recipe 3: Stuffed Dates with Almonds
## Sunnah Starter - Break Your Fast Right

**Time:** 5 minutes | **Serves:** 8 | **Calories:** 120

### Ingredients:
- 12 Medjool dates
- 24 raw almonds
- 2 tbsp honey
- Pinch of sea salt
- Cinnamon (optional)

### Instructions:
1. Make a small slit in each date
2. Remove pit if present
3. Insert 2 almonds into each date
4. Arrange on plate
5. Drizzle with honey
6. Sprinkle with salt and cinnamon

**Hadith:** "When one of you breaks his fast, let him break it with dates, for they are blessed." - Tirmidhi

# Recipe 4: Baked Salmon with Vegetables
## Brain & Body Fuel

**Time:** 30 minutes | **Serves:** 4 | **Calories:** 380

### Ingredients:
- 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lemon
- Fresh dill or parsley
- Salt & pepper

### Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C)
2. Arrange vegetables on baking sheet
3. Drizzle with 2 tbsp olive oil, season
4. Roast 10 minutes
5. Place salmon on top of vegetables
6. Brush with remaining oil, garlic, lemon juice
7. Bake 12-15 minutes until salmon flakes
8. Garnish with fresh herbs

**Tip:** Omega-3s help with focus during long Taraweeh nights!

# Recipe 5: Chickpea & Spinach Curry
## Plant Power - Vegetarian Favorite

**Time:** 25 minutes | **Serves:** 6 | **Calories:** 280

### Ingredients:
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained
- 4 cups fresh spinach
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp curry powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro

### Instructions:
1. Sauté onion until golden (5 min)
2. Add garlic, ginger, spices - stir 1 minute
3. Add chickpeas and coconut milk
4. Simmer 10 minutes
5. Stir in spinach until wilted
6. Season to taste
7. Serve over rice with cilantro

**Tip:** High fiber keeps you full without feeling heavy!

# Recipe 6: Yogurt Parfait with Fruits
## Refreshing Finish

**Time:** 10 minutes | **Serves:** 4 | **Calories:** 220

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups Greek yogurt
- 1 cup mixed berries
- ½ cup granola
- 2 tbsp honey
- Fresh mint leaves
- Chia seeds (optional)

### Instructions:
1. Layer yogurt in glasses or bowls
2. Add layer of berries
3. Sprinkle granola
4. Repeat layers
5. Drizzle honey on top
6. Garnish with mint and chia seeds

**Tip:** Probiotics aid digestion after a long fast!

# Recipe 7: Quinoa Tabbouleh
## Middle Eastern Classic Made Healthy

**Time:** 20 minutes | **Serves:** 6 | **Calories:** 190

### Ingredients:
- 1 cup quinoa, cooked
- 2 cups fresh parsley, chopped
- ½ cup fresh mint, chopped
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 3 green onions, sliced
- ¼ cup olive oil
- Juice of 2 lemons
- Salt & pepper

### Instructions:
1. Cook quinoa according to package, let cool
2. Chop all herbs and vegetables finely
3. Combine everything in large bowl
4. Dress with olive oil and lemon juice
5. Season generously with salt & pepper
6. Refrigerate 30 min before serving

# Recipe 8: Hummus with Veggie Sticks
## Perfect for Suhoor or Iftar

**Time:** 10 minutes | **Serves:** 8 | **Calories:** 150

### Ingredients:
- 1 can chickpeas
- ¼ cup tahini
- 2 cloves garlic
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp cumin
- Salt to taste
- Carrots, celery, cucumber for dipping

### Instructions:
1. Blend chickpeas, tahini, garlic, lemon juice
2. Add olive oil while blending
3. Season with cumin and salt
4. Add water if too thick
5. Drizzle with olive oil and paprika
6. Serve with fresh vegetable sticks

# Suhoor Tips

The pre-dawn meal is crucial! Focus on:

1. **Complex carbs:** Oatmeal, whole grain bread
2. **Protein:** Eggs, yogurt, cheese
3. **Healthy fats:** Avocado, nuts, olive oil
4. **Hydration:** Water, herbal tea, fruits

**Avoid:** Salty foods, caffeine, sugary foods
