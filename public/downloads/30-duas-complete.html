🤲 30 Daily Duas for Ramadan

One powerful dua for each day. Memorize it. Understand it. Live it.

Day 1 — Guidance & Self-Sufficiency

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى

Allahumma inni as'aluka al-huda wa al-tuqa wa al-'afafa wa al-ghina

"O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity, and self-sufficiency."

Start the month asking for the foundations of a good life.

Day 2 — Good in Both Worlds

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Rabbana atina fi al-dunya hasanatan wa fi al-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhab al-nar

"Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the Fire."

The Prophet ﷺ made this dua frequently. It covers everything.

Day 3 — Relief from Worry

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ

Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min al-hammi wa al-hazan

"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief."

Let go of anxiety. Give your worries to Allah.

Day 4 — Ease in Affairs

رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي

Rabbi ishrah li sadri wa yassir li amri

"My Lord, expand my chest and ease my affairs."

Musa's (AS) dua before speaking to Pharaoh. Ask for ease in everything.

Day 5 — Help in Worship

اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَى ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ

Allahumma a'inni 'ala dhikrika wa shukrika wa husni 'ibadatik

"O Allah, help me remember You, be grateful to You, and worship You well."

The Prophet ﷺ taught this to Mu'adh (RA). The core of worship.

Day 6 — Knowledge, Provision & Acceptance

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا

Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an wa rizqan tayyiban wa 'amalan mutaqabbala

"O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, good provision, and accepted deeds."

Made after Fajr. Covers knowledge, wealth, and deeds.

🤲 30 Daily Duas (Days 7-12)

Day 7 — Protection of Guidance

رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحْمَةً

Rabbana la tuzigh qulubana ba'da idh hadaytana wa hab lana min ladunka rahmah

"Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy."

Protect your guidance. Hearts can change — ask Allah to keep yours firm.

Day 8 — Complete Forgiveness

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي كُلَّهُ دِقَّهُ وَجِلَّهُ وَأَوَّلَهُ وَآخِرَهُ

Allahumma ighfir li dhanbi kullahu diqqahu wa jillahu wa awwalahu wa akhirah

"O Allah, forgive all my sins — small and great, first and last."

Leave no sin behind. Ask for complete forgiveness.

Day 9 — Paradise & Protection from Fire

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْجَنَّةَ وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ النَّارِ

Allahumma inni as'aluka al-jannah wa a'udhu bika min al-nar

"O Allah, I ask You for Paradise and seek refuge from the Fire."

Simple. Powerful. The ultimate ask.

Day 10 — Rectify My Religion

اللَّهُمَّ أَصْلِحْ لِي دِينِي الَّذِي هُوَ عِصْمَةُ أَمْرِي

Allahumma aslih li dini alladhi huwa 'ismatu amri

"O Allah, set right my religion which is the safeguard of my affairs."

Your deen protects everything else. Ask for it to be rectified.

Day 11 — Gratitude

رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِي أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ الَّتِي أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيَّ

Rabbi awzi'ni an ashkura ni'mataka allati an'amta 'alayya

"My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favor which You bestowed upon me."

Sulayman's (AS) dua. Gratitude unlocks more blessings.

Day 12 — Protection from Evil

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا عَمِلْتُ وَمِنْ شَرِّ مَا لَمْ أَعْمَلْ

Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min sharri ma 'amiltu wa min sharri ma lam a'mal

"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done and what I have not done."

Protection from consequences of actions — done and undone.

🤲 30 Daily Duas (Days 13-18)

Day 13 — All Good

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنَ الْخَيْرِ كُلِّهِ عَاجِلِهِ وَآجِلِهِ

Allahumma inni as'aluka min al-khayri kullihi 'ajilihi wa ajilihi

"O Allah, I ask You for all good — immediate and in the future."

Comprehensive. Covers all good, now and later.

Day 14 — Righteous Family

رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ

Rabbana hab lana min azwajina wa dhurriyyatina qurrata a'yun

"Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and offspring comfort to our eyes."

For family. Ask Allah for righteous spouse and children.

Day 15 — HALFWAY! Good Endings

اللَّهُمَّ أَحْسِنْ عَاقِبَتَنَا فِي الْأُمُورِ كُلِّهَا

Allahumma ahsin 'aqibatana fi al-umuri kulliha

"O Allah, make good the end of all our affairs."

Halfway through Ramadan. Ask for a strong finish!

Day 16 — Against Laziness

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ

Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min al-'ajzi wa al-kasal

"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from inability and laziness."

Fight the mid-Ramadan slump. Ask for energy and motivation.

Day 17 — Guidance & Steadfastness

اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنِي وَسَدِّدْنِي

Allahumma ihdini wa saddidni

"O Allah, guide me and keep me on the right path."

Short and powerful. Guidance + steadfastness.

Day 18 — Increase in Knowledge

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

Rabbi zidni 'ilma

"My Lord, increase me in knowledge."

The ONLY thing Allah told the Prophet ﷺ to ask for more of in the Quran.

🤲 30 Daily Duas (Days 19-24)

Day 19 — Love of Allah

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ حُبَّكَ وَحُبَّ مَنْ يُحِبُّكَ

Allahumma inni as'aluka hubbaka wa hubba man yuhibbuk

"O Allah, I ask You for Your love and the love of those who love You."

Love of Allah is the highest station. Ask for it.

Day 20 — Reaching Laylatul Qadr

اللَّهُمَّ بَلِّغْنَا لَيْلَةَ الْقَدْرِ

Allahumma ballighna laylat al-qadr

"O Allah, let us reach Laylatul Qadr."

The last 10 nights begin! Ask to reach them alive and well.

⭐ Day 21 — THE Laylatul Qadr Dua

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul 'afwa fa'fu 'anni

"O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me."

The Prophet ﷺ taught this to Aisha (RA) specifically for Laylatul Qadr. REPEAT EVERY ODD NIGHT.

Day 22 — Steadfastness

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الثَّبَاتَ فِي الْأَمْرِ

Allahumma inni as'aluka al-thabata fi al-amr

"O Allah, I ask You for steadfastness in my affairs."

Stay strong. Don't fade in the final stretch.

⭐ Day 23 — Repeat Laylatul Qadr Dua

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul 'afwa fa'fu 'anni

"O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me."

Odd night! Go all in with your duas tonight.

Day 24 — Best Ending

اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْ خَيْرَ عُمُرِي آخِرَهُ وَخَيْرَ عَمَلِي خَوَاتِمَهُ

Allahumma ij'al khayra 'umuri akhirahu wa khayra 'amali khawatimah

"O Allah, make the best of my life its end, and the best of my deeds their conclusion."

The ending matters most. Finish Ramadan strong.

🤲 30 Daily Duas (Days 25-30)

⭐ Day 25 — Repeat Laylatul Qadr Dua

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul 'afwa fa'fu 'anni

"O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me."

Odd night! Many scholars say 25th is likely Laylatul Qadr.

Day 26 — Freedom from Fire

اللَّهُمَّ أَعْتِقْ رَقَبَتِي مِنَ النَّارِ

Allahumma a'tiq raqabati min al-nar

"O Allah, free my neck from the Fire."

Every night of Ramadan, Allah frees people from the Fire. Ask to be one of them.

⭐⭐ Day 27 — MOST LIKELY LAYLATUL QADR

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul 'afwa fa'fu 'anni

"O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me."

GO ALL IN TONIGHT. Make every dua you've ever wanted. Don't sleep!

Day 28 — Acceptance

اللَّهُمَّ تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

Allahumma taqabbal minna innaka anta al-sami' al-'alim

"O Allah, accept from us. You are the All-Hearing, All-Knowing."

Ibrahim and Ismail's (AS) dua when building the Ka'bah. Ask for acceptance.

⭐ Day 29 — Last Odd Night!

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul 'afwa fa'fu 'anni

"O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me."

Last odd night. Could be Laylatul Qadr. Don't miss it!

Day 30 — Among the Saved

اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنَا مِنْ عُتَقَائِكَ مِنَ النَّارِ

Allahumma ij'alna min 'utaqa'ika min al-nar

"O Allah, make us among those You have freed from the Fire."

As Ramadan ends, ask to be among the saved. Ameen!
