⭐ About Laylatul Qadr

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Search for Laylatul Qadr in the odd nights of the last ten nights of Ramadan."

This one night is better than 1,000 months (83+ years). Don't miss the odd nights!

✅ Pre-Ramadan Preparation

Don't wait for Ramadan to start preparing. The Sahaba used to prepare months in advance.

🤲 Spiritual Preparation

  • Make tawbah (repentance) — enter Ramadan clean
  • Forgive everyone who wronged you
  • Ask forgiveness from anyone YOU wronged
  • Settle any debts you can
  • Make a list of bad habits to break
  • Make a list of good habits to build
  • Set your Quran goal
  • Identify your weak spots

📦 Practical Preparation

  • Stock up on dates, water, healthy foods
  • Meal plan for the first week
  • Clear your schedule where possible
  • Set up a prayer space at home
  • Download Quran app / get physical Quran ready
  • Find your masjid's Taraweeh schedule
  • Calculate your Zakat
  • Prepare kids for Ramadan

🤲 30 Daily Duas

One powerful dua for each day of Ramadan. Memorize it. Understand it. Live it.

Day 1

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى

Allahumma inni as'aluka al-huda wa al-tuqa wa al-'afafa wa al-ghina

"O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity, and self-sufficiency."

Day 2

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Rabbana atina fi al-dunya hasanatan wa fi al-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhab al-nar

"Our Lord, give us good in this world and the Hereafter, and protect us from the Fire."

Day 3

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ

Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min al-hammi wa al-hazan

"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief."

Day 4

رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي

Rabbi ishrah li sadri wa yassir li amri

"My Lord, expand my chest and ease my affairs."

Day 5

اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَى ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ

Allahumma a'inni 'ala dhikrika wa shukrika wa husni 'ibadatik

"O Allah, help me remember You, be grateful to You, and worship You well."

🤲 30 Daily Duas (continued)

Day 6

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا

Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an wa rizqan tayyiban wa 'amalan mutaqabbala

"O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, good provision, and accepted deeds."

Day 7

رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا

Rabbana la tuzigh qulubana ba'da idh hadaytana

"Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us."

Day 8

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي كُلَّهُ

Allahumma ighfir li dhanbi kullahu

"O Allah, forgive all my sins."

Day 9

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْجَنَّةَ وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ النَّارِ

Allahumma inni as'aluka al-jannah wa a'udhu bika min al-nar

"O Allah, I ask You for Paradise and seek refuge from the Fire."

Day 10

اللَّهُمَّ أَصْلِحْ لِي دِينِي

Allahumma aslih li dini

"O Allah, set right my religion."

⭐ The Last 10 Nights

These nights are worth more than a lifetime. Treat them that way.

THE Dua for Laylatul Qadr

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul 'afwa fa'fu 'anni

"O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me."

The Prophet ﷺ taught this to Aisha (RA) specifically for these nights. Repeat it constantly.

🍽️ Meal Planning

Suhoor Tips (Energy That Lasts)

✅ Best Foods

  • Oatmeal with dates & honey
  • Eggs + whole grain toast
  • Greek yogurt with nuts
  • Banana smoothie with oats
  • Avocado toast

❌ Avoid

  • Sugary cereals (crash by noon)
  • Heavy fried foods (sluggish)
  • Too much salt (thirsty)
  • Caffeine (dehydrates)

Iftar Tips

  • Start with: Dates + water (Sunnah)
  • Then: Light soup or salad
  • Main: Balanced meal — don't overeat!
  • Hydrate: Water between Iftar and Suhoor

Eid Day Sunnah

  1. Wake up early
  2. Take a bath (ghusl)
  3. Wear your best clothes
  4. Eat dates before prayer (odd number)
  5. Say takbeer on the way to prayer
  6. Go one route, return another
  7. Pray Eid salah
  8. Give Eid greetings: "Taqabbal Allahu minna wa minkum"

Eid Takbeer

اللهُ أَكْبَرُ اللهُ أَكْبَرُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَاللهُ أَكْبَرُ اللهُ أَكْبَرُ وَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ

Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, La ilaha illa Allah. Wa Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, wa lillahi al-hamd.

🔄 After Ramadan: Keep the Momentum

The real test isn't Ramadan — it's Shawwal and beyond.

Don't Lose Your Gains

  • Continue waking for Fajr on time
  • Keep daily Quran reading (even 1 page)
  • Fast Mondays & Thursdays (or 3 days/month)
  • Stay connected to the masjid
  • Keep giving regular charity
  • Maintain the dua habit

6 Fasts of Shawwal

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever fasts Ramadan and follows it with six days of Shawwal, it's as if they fasted the entire year."
