🍲 Lentil Soup (Shorbat Adas)
The Prophet's ﷺ Favorite
⏱️ 25 min 👥 Serves 6 🔥 180 cal

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups water or vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp turmeric
  • Salt, pepper, juice of 1 lemon

Instructions:

  1. Sauté onion in olive oil until soft (3 min)
  2. Add garlic, cumin, turmeric - stir 1 minute
  3. Add lentils, carrots, and water
  4. Bring to boil, then simmer 20 minutes
  5. Blend until smooth, add lemon juice, season
💡 Tip: Make a big batch and freeze portions!
🥗 Grilled Chicken Salad
Light & Energizing
⏱️ 20 min 👥 Serves 4 🔥 320 cal

Ingredients:

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • Cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion
  • Dressing: olive oil, lemon juice, honey

Instructions:

  1. Season and grill chicken 6-7 min per side
  2. Let rest, then slice
  3. Arrange greens with vegetables
  4. Top with chicken, drizzle dressing
💡 Tip: Prep chicken night before for faster iftar!
🌴 Stuffed Dates with Almonds
Sunnah Starter
⏱️ 5 min 👥 Serves 8 🔥 120 cal

Ingredients:

  • 12 Medjool dates
  • 24 raw almonds
  • Honey, sea salt, cinnamon

Instructions:

  1. Slit dates and remove pits
  2. Insert 2 almonds in each
  3. Drizzle honey, sprinkle salt & cinnamon
"When one of you breaks his fast, let him break it with dates, for they are blessed." - Tirmidhi
🐟 Baked Salmon with Vegetables
Brain & Body Fuel
⏱️ 30 min 👥 Serves 4 🔥 380 cal

Ingredients:

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • Broccoli, bell peppers, zucchini
  • Olive oil, garlic, lemon, herbs

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C)
  2. Roast vegetables 10 minutes
  3. Add salmon on top, brush with oil & garlic
  4. Bake 12-15 minutes until done
💡 Tip: Omega-3s help focus during Taraweeh!
🥘 Chickpea & Spinach Curry
Plant Power
⏱️ 25 min 👥 Serves 6 🔥 280 cal

Ingredients:

  • 2 cans chickpeas
  • 4 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • Onion, garlic, ginger, curry spices

Instructions:

  1. Sauté onion, add garlic & spices
  2. Add chickpeas and coconut milk
  3. Simmer 10 min, stir in spinach
  4. Serve over rice
🍨 Yogurt Parfait with Fruits
Refreshing Finish
⏱️ 10 min 👥 Serves 4 🔥 220 cal

Ingredients:

  • Greek yogurt, mixed berries
  • Granola, honey, mint

Instructions:

  1. Layer yogurt, berries, granola
  2. Repeat layers
  3. Top with honey & mint
💡 Tip: Probiotics aid digestion after fasting!
