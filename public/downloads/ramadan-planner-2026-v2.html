🤲 30 Daily Duas - Days 1-6

Day 1
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى
Allahumma inni as'aluka al-huda wa al-tuqa wa al-'afafa wa al-ghina
"O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity, and self-sufficiency."
Day 2
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhab an-nar
"Our Lord, give us good in this world and in the Hereafter, and protect us from the Fire."
Day 3
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ
Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min al-hammi wal-hazan
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief."
Day 4
رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي
Rabbi-shrah li sadri wa yassir li amri
"My Lord, expand my chest and ease my affairs."
Day 5
اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَى ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ
Allahumma a'inni 'ala dhikrika wa shukrika wa husni 'ibadatik
"O Allah, help me remember You, thank You, and worship You properly."
Day 6
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا
Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an wa rizqan tayyiban wa 'amalan mutaqabbala
"O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, good provision, and accepted deeds."

🤲 30 Daily Duas - Days 7-12

Day 7
رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا
Rabbana la tuzigh qulubana ba'da idh hadaytana
"Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us."
Day 8
اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي كُلَّهُ دِقَّهُ وَجِلَّهُ
Allahumma-ghfir li dhanbi kullahu diqqahu wa jillahu
"O Allah, forgive all my sins, small and great."
Day 9
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْجَنَّةَ وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ النَّارِ
Allahumma inni as'aluka al-jannata wa a'udhu bika min an-nar
"O Allah, I ask You for Paradise and seek refuge from the Fire."
Day 10
اللَّهُمَّ أَصْلِحْ لِي دِينِي الَّذِي هُوَ عِصْمَةُ أَمْرِي
Allahumma aslih li dini alladhi huwa 'ismatu amri
"O Allah, set right my religion which is the safeguard of my affairs."
Day 11
رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِي أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ
Rabbi awzi'ni an ashkura ni'mataka
"My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your blessings."
Day 12
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا عَمِلْتُ
Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min sharri ma 'amiltu
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done."

🤲 30 Daily Duas - Days 13-18

Day 13
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنَ الْخَيْرِ كُلِّهِ
Allahumma inni as'aluka min al-khayri kullihi
"O Allah, I ask You for all that is good."
Day 14
رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ
Rabbana hab lana min azwajina wa dhurriyyatina qurrata a'yun
"Our Lord, grant us spouses and children who are the comfort of our eyes."
Day 15 - HALFWAY!
اللَّهُمَّ أَحْسِنْ عَاقِبَتَنَا فِي الْأُمُورِ كُلِّهَا
Allahumma ahsin 'aqibatana fil-umuri kulliha
"O Allah, make the outcome of all our affairs good."
Day 16
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ
Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min al-'ajzi wal-kasal
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from inability and laziness."
Day 17
اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنِي وَسَدِّدْنِي
Allahumma-hdini wa saddidni
"O Allah, guide me and keep me on the right path."
Day 18
رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا
Rabbi zidni 'ilma
"My Lord, increase me in knowledge."

🤲 30 Daily Duas - Days 19-24

Day 19
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ حُبَّكَ وَحُبَّ مَنْ يُحِبُّكَ
Allahumma inni as'aluka hubbaka wa hubba man yuhibbuk
"O Allah, I ask You for Your love and the love of those who love You."
Day 20 - Last 10 Begin!
اللَّهُمَّ بَلِّغْنَا لَيْلَةَ الْقَدْرِ
Allahumma ballighna laylat al-qadr
"O Allah, let us reach Laylatul Qadr."
Day 21 ⭐ ODD NIGHT
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي
Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul-'afwa fa'fu 'anni
"O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me."
Day 22
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الثَّبَاتَ فِي الْأَمْرِ
Allahumma inni as'aluka ath-thabata fil-amr
"O Allah, I ask You for steadfastness in my affairs."
Day 23 ⭐ ODD NIGHT
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي
Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul-'afwa fa'fu 'anni
"O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me."
Day 24
اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْ خَيْرَ عُمُرِي آخِرَهُ
Allahumma-j'al khayra 'umuri akhirahu
"O Allah, make the best of my life its end."

اللَّهُمَّ تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
Allahumma taqabbal minna innaka antas-sami'ul-'alim
"O Allah, accept from us. You are the All-Hearing, All-Knowing."
Day 29 ⭐ LAST ODD NIGHT

Allahumma-j'alna min 'utaqa'ika min an-nar
"O Allah, make us among those You have freed from the Fire."
